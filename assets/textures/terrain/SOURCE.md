# Terrain textures (Poly Haven, CC0)
2K PBR sets from https://polyhaven.com (CC0), fetched 2026-10-11 by `tools/assets/ph_fetch.py`: diffuse (sRGB), normal (OpenGL, Godot convention), ARM (R ambient occlusion, G roughness, B metal), displacement (height for the height blend).
Layer order matches `assets/terrain/rift_lake/splat_a.png` RGBA then `splat_b.png` RGB:
1 withered_grass (dry grass) 2 red_mud_stones (red soil) 3 dark_rock (volcanic rock) 4 coast_sand_01 (shore sand)
5 brown_mud_02 (wet mud) 6 dry_river_pebbles (cobble bed) 7 mud_cracked_dry_riverbed_002 (cracked mud).
Tiling and tints: docs/DESIGN.md 7.2. Sky: ../../sky/qwantani_late_afternoon_4k.hdr (Poly Haven, CC0).
