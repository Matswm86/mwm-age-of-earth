## Opening intro (scenes/Intro.tscn, assets/intro/)

Fetched 2026-10-11. Build scripts: `tools/intro/` (bake_dem.py, bake_intro_assets.py, bake_paleo.py, hero_clips.py, s2fetch.py, render_earth.py).

**What is real data and what is invented.** The intro starts from modern imagery (2004-2016) and edits it toward the Earth of ~7 million years ago (globe) and ~4.2 million years ago (the Kanapoi close-up) with `tools/intro/bake_paleo.py`. Every edit below is a design inference made for the intro, not a reconstruction:
- Globe (~7 Ma): northern-summer (July) Blue Marble north of ~28 N so North America and Eurasia carry no snow; Greenland ice cut back to a patchy east-central cap (extent invented); Sahara and Sahel recoloured to a patchy tan-green that greens to the south, Arabia and East Africa greener (patch pattern is procedural noise); lakes in the Chad basin drawn below the 280 m contour of the Scotese & Wright present-day PaleoDEM (size invented for 7 Ma); Central American Seaway opened as a channel across the Darien (course invented, placed where the Scotese & Wright 5 Ma and 10 Ma PaleoDEMs leave their gap); Mediterranean kept full; coastlines otherwise modern. The Scotese Map 4 (6.3 Ma) has no PaleoDEM grid in the Zenodo record (the 6-minute and 1-degree sets jump from Map 3, 5 Ma, to Map 5, 10 Ma); Map 4 was used only as a visual reference from the PALEOMAP Neogene atlas PDF.
- Rift (~4.2 Ma): today's Lake Turkana, Mount Kulal (volcano, ~2.4 Ma and younger) and South Island removed (filled from the surrounding land); **Lake Lonyumun drawn by hand**: water below 560 m inside a hand-drawn outline in the southern basin, shore at the Kanapoi site. The lake's real outline is not known (it existed ~4.2-4.1 Ma for ~100 kyr); its shape, size and depth here are invented. The ancestral Kerio River is a drawn meandering course along today's Kerio valley with a three-arm delta at Kanapoi (invented). Vegetation recoloured to a mosaic of closed woodland near water, shrubs and grassland (after the Kanapoi habitat literature; pattern procedural). Modern dirt tracks softened with a median filter.
- Dive target: Kanapoi, 2.3167 N 36.0667 E, Paleobiology Database collection 22234 (https://paleobiodb.org/classic/basicCollectionSearch?collection_no=22234), after Patterson, B., Behrensmeyer, A.K. & Sill, W.D. 1970, Geology and fauna of a new Pliocene locality in north-western Kenya, Nature 226:918-921.
- The ground scenes after the clouds use the game's own map (West Turkana relief, see "Terrain data"), not Kanapoi relief.
