## Opening intro (scenes/Intro.tscn, assets/intro/)

Fetched 2026-10-11. Build scripts: `tools/intro/` (bake_dem.py, bake_intro_assets.py, s2fetch.py, render_earth.py).
Coastlines, deserts, forests and lakes are modern (2004-2016 imagery) and stand in for the Earth of 7-10 million years ago; that likeness is unverified.

- **Earth albedo:** NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, December 2004, with topography and bathymetry (NASA Earth Observatory, Reto Stöckli). Files:
  https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73909/world.topo.bathy.200412.3x21600x10800.jpg (global, resampled to 16k and 8k),
  https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73909/world.topo.bathy.200412.3x21600x21600.C1.jpg and `...C2.jpg` (500 m East Africa crop).
  No city-lights layer is used.
- **Clouds:** NASA Blue Marble clouds, https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_8192.tif
- **Ocean mask / relief:** NASA Visible Earth renderings of GEBCO_08 bathymetry and topography,
  https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73963/gebco_08_rev_bath_21600x10800.png (ocean mask, in game) and
  https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73934/gebco_08_rev_elev_21600x10800.png (bump map, Blender script only).
- NASA licence: per the NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/), NASA content including texture maps "generally are not subject to copyright in the United States". Credit NASA; no NASA endorsement is implied. GEBCO: "The GEBCO Grid is placed in the public domain and may be used free of charge" (https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry-data, read for the current grid; that GEBCO_08 carries the same terms is assumed, unverified).
- **Rift close-up imagery:** Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX IT Services GmbH, https://s2maps.eu (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), licence Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (licence read from the layer abstract in https://tiles.maps.eox.at/wmts/1.0.0/WMTSCapabilities.xml, layer `s2cloudless`). Changes: mosaicked, colour-matched to Blue Marble. Only the 2016 layer is CC BY; later years are not, and are not used. Faint dirt tracks visible at 10 m are modern.
- **Rift relief:** produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 (same tiles and notice as the Rift Lake map above; tiles N02-N05, E034-E036 from https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.amazonaws.com/).
- **Stars:** Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised ed. (Hoffleit & Warren 1991), via CDS VizieR catalogue V/50, https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/V/50/ . Positions, magnitudes and colours are factual data; acknowledgement as CDS asks: this game uses the VizieR catalogue access tool, CDS, Strasbourg, France.
- **Atmosphere constants:** Rayleigh, Mie and ozone coefficients after Bruneton & Neyret 2008 and Hillaire 2020; shaders written for this project.
